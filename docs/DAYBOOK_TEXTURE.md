## What this is for

The scholarship gives the game its *arguments*. The diary gives it the register in
which a life is actually lived: rent, illness, weather, who called, what was lent and
not returned. `WRITING_GUIDE.md` asks every scene to surface something specific and
real; this file is where the small specific real things are.

Two cautions. These are **summaries of entries**, not the entries themselves — verify
wording in Fenton before quoting. And the diary's silences are evidence too: where it
goes quiet is a fact about Dee, not a gap to be filled with invention.

## How to use this in a revision pass

1. Take a node from `content/choices.json` and its act.
2. Find the matching topic section above and read for a *concrete particular* —
   a sum of money, an illness, a named caller, a piece of weather.
3. Put the particular in the **scene text**, not the option labels. Options stay
   10–20 words and carry the decision; the scene carries the world.
4. Verify the wording in Fenton before quoting anything as Dee's own phrasing.
5. Re-run `node ../../tools/lint.mjs dee` — the ceiling on scene length is 60 words,
   and adding texture is exactly how prose drifts past it.
